모범 답안: Marc wondered whether the birds in line were more fearful because they didn’t know what their flockmates were doing. 채점 포인트: ① 지정한 wondered whether와 뒤의 whether절 ② 이유를 나타내는 표현(because ...)으로 ‘알지 못했기 때문에’를 나타냄 ③ what their flockmates were doing(의문사절). 시제는 과거로 맞춥니다.
