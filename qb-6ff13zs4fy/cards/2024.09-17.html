① cheetahFirst, there’s no animal faster on land than the cheetah.에서 언급되었습니다.
② falconSecond, the falcon is one of the fastest birds.에서 언급되었습니다.
④ swordfishThird, the swordfish is among the fastest fish in the sea.에서 언급되었습니다.
⑤ dragonflyLastly, the dragonfly is considered the world’s fastest flying insect.에서 언급되었습니다.
